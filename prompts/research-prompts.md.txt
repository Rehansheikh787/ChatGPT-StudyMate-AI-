# 🔍 Research Prompts

## Prompt 1

Act as a Principal Product Manager and Consumer Psychologist.
Research Objective:
Understand the unmet needs, motivations, behaviors, and Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) of high-school students (13–18 years old) in Tier-1 cities in India who use AI tools such as ChatGPT.
Business Goal:
Increase average sessions per student per day to more than 5.
Conduct a deep JTBD and behavioral analysis.
Research Areas:
User Segmentation
Segment high-school students into meaningful behavioral groups.
Examples:
Academic achievers
Exam-focused students
Competitive exam aspirants
Creators
Curious learners
Social learners
Productivity seekers
Jobs-To-Be-Done Analysis
For each segment identify:
Functional Jobs
What task are they trying to complete?
Emotional Jobs
How do they want to feel?
Social Jobs
How do they want to be perceived?
Current Alternatives
What tools or methods do students currently use instead of ChatGPT?
Examples:
Google Search
YouTube
Friends
Teachers
Coaching classes
WhatsApp groups
Educational apps
Friction Analysis
Identify:
What prevents students from opening ChatGPT multiple times per day?
What causes abandonment?
What limits repeat engagement?
Habit Formation Analysis
Using behavioral frameworks such as:
Hook Model
Fogg Behavior Model
Habit Loop
Identify:
Triggers
Motivations
Rewards
Retention mechanisms
Opportunity Discovery
Identify:
Underserved needs
Unmet needs
Latent needs
High-frequency use cases
Prioritization
Rank opportunities using:
Frequency
User Value
Engagement Potential
Strategic Alignment with OpenAI
Output Format:
User Segments
JTBD Table
Pain Points
Habit Formation Insights
Opportunity Areas
Prioritized Opportunity Matrix
Recommended Focus Area
