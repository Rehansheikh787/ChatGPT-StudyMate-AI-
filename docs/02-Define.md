# 🎯 Define

## Objective

The Define phase focused on identifying the highest-impact problem to solve based on user needs, business objectives, and AI capabilities.

Rather than solving every challenge students face, this phase prioritized one problem that could deliver the greatest value while remaining feasible for an MVP.

---

# Problem Statement

Students preparing for competitive examinations often struggle to maintain consistent study habits because their learning journey is fragmented across multiple platforms.

While ChatGPT provides excellent answers to individual questions, it does not proactively guide students through structured learning or help them stay engaged over time.

---

# Product Goal

Transform ChatGPT from a reactive question-answering assistant into a proactive AI learning companion.

The product should help students:

- Stay consistent
- Study smarter
- Revise effectively
- Build long-term learning habits

---

# Success Criteria

The solution should:

- Increase daily engagement
- Improve study consistency
- Reduce learning friction
- Personalize the learning experience
- Encourage long-term retention

---

# Business Objective

From a product perspective, increasing meaningful daily engagement strengthens user retention and positions ChatGPT as a primary learning platform rather than an occasional homework assistant.

---

# Target Users

## Primary Users

- Class 9–12 Students
- JEE Aspirants
- NEET Aspirants

## Secondary Users

- Parents
- Teachers

---

# User Needs

Students need:

- Personalized study plans
- Daily guidance
- Intelligent revision
- Motivation
- Progress tracking
- Instant doubt solving

---

# Product Principles

This product is designed around five principles:

1. Personalization
2. Simplicity
3. Proactive Assistance
4. Continuous Learning
5. Trustworthy AI

---

# Prioritization

Several opportunities were identified during Discovery.

The following opportunities were prioritized for the MVP:

| Opportunity | Priority |
|-------------|----------|
| Personalized Study Plan | High |
| AI Tutor | High |
| Smart Revision | High |
| Daily Motivation | Medium |
| Parent Dashboard | Low |
| Peer Learning | Low |

---

# Why These Features?

These features were selected because they:

- Solve the highest-frequency user problems
- Can leverage AI effectively
- Improve daily engagement
- Are feasible within an MVP scope

---

# Risks

Potential risks include:

- AI hallucinations
- Student overdependence on AI
- Incorrect study recommendations
- Privacy concerns

Mitigation strategies include human verification for critical content, transparent AI limitations, and user feedback loops.

---

# Outcome

The Define phase established a clear product direction focused on building an AI-powered study companion that encourages consistent learning through personalized guidance and proactive engagement.

These decisions guided the solution design in the Develop phase.
