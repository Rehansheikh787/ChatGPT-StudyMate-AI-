Create a Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD) map for the following product challenge:

Product:
ChatGPT for High School Students in Tier-1 Indian Cities

Business Goal:
Increase average sessions per student per day to more than 5.

Primary Persona:
JEE/NEET Aspirant (Age 16–18)

Persona Context:
- Studies 6–8 hours daily
- Attends coaching classes
- Uses PhysicsWallah, YouTube, WhatsApp and ChatGPT
- Faces intense academic competition
- Experiences exam anxiety
- Needs instant doubt solving

Organize the JTBDs into the following hierarchy:

Main Job
→ Functional Jobs
→ Emotional Jobs
→ Social Jobs

For each job include:
- Desired Outcome
- Current Alternative
- Existing Pain Point

Structure the visualization as a JTBD tree.

Examples:

Main Job:
Crack JEE/NEET with confidence

Functional Jobs:
- Understand difficult concepts
- Solve numerical problems
- Revise efficiently
- Identify weak topics

Emotional Jobs:
- Reduce anxiety
- Feel prepared
- Gain confidence

Social Jobs:
- Meet parental expectations
- Keep up with peers
- Achieve academic recognition

Use a clean product management workshop style.
